New Venue

Booking a venue that's new to us

We advance every new room before event day. Our crew calls the venue coordinator to confirm the load-in window, the door or dock, any elevator, and where the power is, and asks about house rules for fog, haze and sound levels. The plan for your event is built around those answers, and the certificate of insurance goes to the venue ahead of load-in.

Then the event runs like it does at any venue on this page: we deliver, set up, test and pick up, with an on-site technician for the services that call for one. Wireless microphones run our AI-powered De-Feedback system, so toasts and speeches stay clear in rooms we are seeing for the first time.

What We Bring

Services at these venues

Sound systems and wireless microphones, event lighting and uplighting, DJ services, projectors and screens, Dancing on the Clouds, monogram projection and live streaming. See them in action on our work page and in the photo gallery.

FAQ

Venue questions

Have you worked at my venue before?
Search the list on this page. It covers the event venues on our booking records since late 2020, grouped by city, and we have worked many more rooms since we started in 2001. If your venue is here, our crew has already loaded in, found the power and set up a show in that room.
What if my venue is new to you?
We will advance it before your event. We call the venue coordinator to confirm the load-in window, the door or dock, the elevator and the power, and we plan the setup around the room. New venues are part of every season for us.
Do you send a certificate of insurance to the venue?
Yes. Dallas Event Audio is insured, and we send a certificate of insurance to any venue that requires one, ahead of load-in.
Can you run Dancing on the Clouds at my venue?
Venues marked Clouds on this list have had our Dancing on the Clouds low lying fog for a first dance. For any other venue, we confirm the venue's fog policy and its smoke detector setup with the coordinator before the quote goes out.
Do you coordinate load-in and setup with the venue?
Yes. Our crew delivers, sets up, tests and picks up every booking, with an on-site technician for the services that call for one, and we work out the timing with the venue coordinator so the room is ready before your guests arrive.
