How To Read This

How the equipment list works

This page is the inventory behind every quote we send. It is here so you can see what we own before you call, and so you can tell whether we are the right company for the event you are planning. Each category links to the page that goes into detail on it.

Everything ships as a production package. We deliver it to your venue, build it inside the load-in window, dress the cabling, line-check it, tune it to the room and stay on it for the event, then strike it afterward. Placement, gain structure and having someone on the system during the event are what decide whether a room sounds right, and none of that fits in a road case.

You will notice there are no counts on this page. Our inventory turns over as we buy, sell and replace boxes, and a number printed here would be wrong within a season. So we size the rig to your room on the quote instead. The one exception is the line array, where the box count is part of what you are comparing, and that number is below.

Category 01

PA speakers and powered tops

The boxes that do the work in most rooms. We pick from this shelf by room size, throw distance and how loud the night gets, then add subwoofers underneath where the dance floor needs them. Full detail on PA speaker rental in Dallas-Fort Worth.

01

BassBoss DV12-MK3

Spec 3,200W · 12 in neodymium woofer + 3 in compression driver · 134 dB peak · 45 Hz to 19 kHz

Our loudest single-box top. The BassBoss DV12-MK3 gives you the clarity and the throw of a much bigger rig without the bulk of a full line array, which makes it the box we reach for when a mid-size room has to stay clean at volume. Weddings with a real dance floor, corporate general sessions and live bands all end up on these.

02

Yamaha DZR15

Spec 2,000W bi-amped · 15 in LF + 2 in HF · 139 dB max SPL · 34 Hz to 20 kHz · FIR-X DSP

The 15 inch version of our touring-grade workhorse, reaching down to 34 Hz on its own. A strong PA speaker rental for larger dance floors, live bands and outdoor events where you want headroom in reserve and deeper low end before a subwoofer joins in.

03

Yamaha DZR12

Spec 2,000W bi-amped · 12 in LF + 2 in HF · 139 dB max SPL · 39 Hz to 20 kHz · FIR-X DSP

Same output and the same FIR-X tuning in a 12 inch cabinet, so it flies up on a stick without giving much away. This is the PA speaker rental we quote most often for DFW weddings and corporate productions that want touring-grade sound in a package a venue will not argue about.

04

Electro-Voice ZLX-12P-G2

Spec 1,000W · 12 in woofer + 1.4 in titanium driver · 127 dB max SPL · QuickSmart DSP · Bluetooth

A dependable 1,000 watt powered speaker for corporate meetings, private parties and small to mid-size events across Dallas, Fort Worth and the surrounding metroplex. Onboard DSP and Bluetooth streaming make it quick to deploy for a breakout room or a ceremony that needs one clean source.

05

Electro-Voice ZLX-8P-G2

Spec 1,000W · 8 in woofer + 1.4 in titanium driver · 126 dB peak · built-in 4-channel digital mixer

The compact one. It carries a 4-channel digital mixer inside the cabinet, which makes it a tidy PA speaker rental for smaller DFW events, breakout sessions, cocktail hour, or a fill speaker in a corner the mains do not reach.

06

Flat Panel Audio DML500

Spec Flat panel distributed mode loudspeaker · wide even coverage · low visual profile

A flat panel speaker for rooms where a box on a stick would be in the way or in the photographs. It spreads sound wide and even rather than firing it down a narrow line, which suits galleries, showrooms and ceremonies in tight architectural spaces. More on the Flat Panel Audio DML500.

Category 02

Line array systems

Owned 12 x DAS Audio Aero 12 · 10 x DAS Audio LX218CA subwoofers · flying hardware and ground stack frames in house

This is the one category where the count matters, because when you are comparing line array quotes the number of boxes is part of what you are comparing. We own twelve DAS Audio Aero 12 cabinets and ten LX218CA subwoofers, plus the flying hardware and the ground stack frames, so nothing on a line array job is sub-rented from a warehouse we have never walked into.

A line array goes up when a room is long, tall or loud: ballrooms where the back tables are eighty feet from the stage, outdoor events with no walls to help, night club and festival rooms where the low end has to hold together at the barricade and at the bar. We hang it where the venue allows rigging and ground stack it where it does not.

Category 03

Subwoofers

All BassBoss, all self-powered, all bought because they hold their shape when the room is full. Detail and comparisons on BassBoss subwoofer rental.

01

BassBoss SSP118-MK3

Spec 2,500W · single 18 in neodymium woofer · 130 dB continuous · 30 Hz to 100 Hz

The compact one, and the one that goes out on the widest range of jobs. It is small enough to sit under a DJ booth or behind a plant and still puts 130 dB of continuous output down to 30 Hz into a room, so it is the subwoofer rental we pair with the DZR and ZLX tops for most weddings and parties.

02

BassBoss VS21-MK3

Spec 2,500W · single 21 in neodymium woofer · 138 dB peak · 28 Hz to 100 Hz · cardioid capable

A single 21 inch box built for low end you feel through the floor, down to 28 Hz. Paired up it runs in cardioid mode, which pushes the energy onto the dance floor and keeps it off the stage and the back wall. A favorite subwoofer rental for DFW weddings and parties where the dance floor has to hit.

03

BassBoss ZV28-MK3

Spec 5,000W · dual 18 in neodymium woofers · 137 dB peak · 23 Hz to 90 Hz · cardioid capable · IP65 connectors

The deepest and highest-output subwoofer in our fleet, reaching 23 Hz with 5,000 watts behind it. IP65 rated connectors and cardioid capability make it the box for outdoor festivals, large weddings and high-capacity corporate productions across Dallas-Fort Worth.

04

DAS Audio LX218CA

Spec Dual 18 in powered subwoofer · ground stack or flown · matched to the Aero 12 array

The array subwoofer that goes with the DAS Audio line array, ground stacked in front of the stage or flown with the hangs. It is voiced to sit under the Aero 12 boxes rather than argue with them, which is why we keep the array and its subs as one system.

Category 04

Mixing consoles and stage monitors

Rig Allen & Heath SQ-5 · Soundcraft Ui24R · Soundcraft Ui16 · Yamaha MG12XU · Electro-Voice ZLX-12P monitor wedges

We size the desk to the input list rather than to a package tier. The Allen & Heath SQ-5 is the big digital console, and it comes out when there is a band, a full input list or a show that needs scenes for each segment. The Soundcraft Ui24R and the smaller Soundcraft Ui16 are rack mixers driven from a tablet, which is what we want when front of house has to be a person walking the room instead of a table in the middle of it. The Yamaha MG12XU is the analog desk for simple rooms where a ceremony, a laptop and two microphones is the whole job.

Stage monitors go out with the consoles: Electro-Voice ZLX-12P wedges on the deck for bands and speakers who need to hear themselves, with monitor mixes built during line check and a monitor engineer on larger shows. Send us the stage plot with your inquiry and we will build the monitor world around it.

Category 05

Microphones and wireless systems

Wireless Shure GLXD · SLXD · ULXD · handheld, lapel and headset transmitters

Shure throughout, across the GLXD, SLXD and ULXD families, so we can put the right tier of wireless into a room instead of the only one we own. Handheld transmitters for toasts and questions from the floor, lapel and lavalier for officiants and presenters who move, headset for anyone who needs both hands and a consistent level for an hour.

Wired Shure SM57 · Shure SM58 · Shure Beta 58 · Behringer condenser stereo pair · Pyle PTT tabletop

On the wired side, SM57s on drums, amps and instruments, SM58s for stage vocals, Beta 58s where a vocal needs more reach before feedback, and a Behringer condenser stereo pair for choirs and room capture. The podium rig is a Shure SM58 on a podium stand, so a speaker gets a familiar capsule and we can move the microphone to the person instead of asking the person to lean into a fixed gooseneck. Boardrooms and panels get Pyle push-to-talk tabletop microphones, one per seat, with a clean feed out for the recording. For drums we build the kit to the room, so contact us for custom drum mic kit options.

Every microphone we put in a room runs through our AI-powered De-Feedback system, which finds the frequency that is about to ring and pulls it down before anyone in the room hears a squeal. That is what lets an officiant walk, a presenter step out from the lectern and a panel sit close to the speakers. Detail on feedback-free microphone rentals.

Category 07

Event lighting

Uplighting, dance floor lighting, follow spot, monogram projection and lasers, programmed and operated on the same booking as the audio. Overview on event lighting services.

Uplighting

Battery powered RGBAW-UV fixtures on wireless DMX. The amber, white and UV diodes reach true whites, warm ambers and rich pastels that a plain RGB uplight cannot, so we can match linens, florals and brand colors properly. No outlets and no cable runs, so they go in corners, on columns and outdoors.

Dance floor lighting

Moving head wash, spot and beam fixtures, effect lights, strobes and blinders, rigged on 4 ft or 8 ft truss totems or on truss depending on what the venue allows. Programmed to the night rather than left on an auto setting. Moving heads, strobes and blinders are a design conversation, so contact us for a custom lighting quote and we will set up a consultation with our lighting design team.

Follow spot

Chauvet DJ LED Followspot 75ST, with an operator. For grand entrances, first dances, award presentations and any moment where the room needs to know where to look.

Monogram and gobo projection

Custom monograms, logos and patterns projected onto a wall, a dance floor or a ceiling, run from our Epson projectors rather than a fixed gobo fixture, so the artwork is yours and can be changed up to the day of the event.

Laser systems

Laser lighting for club nights, festivals and large receptions, programmed and operated by our crew. Lasers carry venue rules and permit requirements that change from room to room and city to city, so we review the venue and the permitting before we quote a system. Bring us in early and we will tell you what your room allows.

Pin spots and stage wash

Pin spots on centerpieces and cake tables so the room photographs the way the planner drew it, and stage wash so speakers and bands are lit rather than silhouetted.
Category 08

Fog, haze and special effects

Atmospherics make lighting visible and make a first dance look like the photographs people booked it for. All of it is run by our crew and cleared with the venue before the date.

01

Froggy's Fog Titan 1200

Spec 1,300W heater · 20,000 CFM output · under 10 second heat-up · 5 liter tank · wired remote

Our high output atmospheric fog machine, for filling a large room or a stage quickly. See fog machine rental for how we set output and placement so a venue's smoke detectors stay happy.

02

Chauvet haze machines

Spec Hurricane Haze 2D · High Performance Haze · water based fluid

Haze is the thin, even suspension that makes beams and moving lights visible without anyone seeing a cloud. Water based fluid, which is what most DFW venues will approve when they will not approve fog.

03

Dry ice and low lying fog

Spec Chauvet Nimbus Jr · Chauvet Nimbus · ADJ Entour Ice · Chauvet DJ Cumulus

The dancing on the clouds effect for a first dance. The Nimbus Jr, the Nimbus and the ADJ Entour Ice are dry ice machines, dropping ice into heated water for the dense white cloud that sits on the floor and disappears at knee height. The Chauvet DJ Cumulus gets the same look from distilled water and haze fluid instead of ice, so it runs continuously for photo and video work and for venues that will not allow dry ice on site. Which machine goes out depends on how many moments you want covered. See dancing on the clouds.

04

Cold sparks

Policy Contact us · venue approval and fire department approval required before we quote

Cold spark fountains for entrances, first dances and grand exits. We do not issue a quote for cold sparks until both your venue and the fire department have approved the effect, because that approval is what decides whether it can happen at all. Start the conversation early on cold sparks.

05

CO2 cannons and guns

Spec RGB LED cannons · a pair of handheld LED guns · 20 lb siphon tube cylinders in house

CO2 blasts on a drop, with LED color in the plume. We carry 20 lb siphon tube cylinders in house, and larger cylinders come from a third party gas vendor we coordinate with. See special effects and extras.

Spec Distribution · cable and matting · speaker and lighting stands · venue supplied power

The unglamorous half of the truck, and the half that keeps a venue safe and a floor plan intact. The venue supplies the power, so we walk the room in advance and confirm the circuits are where the rig needs them, rather than finding out on the day. Cable gets dressed and matted instead of taped down in a hurry.

Straight Answers

What we do not rent

Four things we get asked for weekly and cannot help with. Saying so here saves you a phone call.

Individual items

We quote production packages with a crew, so there is no line-item rental of a single speaker or a single microphone. If you need one box for an afternoon, we will tell you and point you somewhere that does it.

Customer pickup

There is no counter to collect from and nothing goes out on the back of a truck you drove here. We deliver, set up, run and strike every booking ourselves.

Permanent installs

Installed systems, building integration and AV consulting for a fixed room are a different trade with different licensing. We are an event company, and we leave installs to the firms that do them full time.

Inflatable and outdoor movie screens

We do not carry inflatable screens or outdoor movie screens of any kind. Our projection is built for indoor and covered spaces where we can control the light.

Do not see what you need on the list?

We build every package custom, and this list is what we own rather than the limit of what we can put in a room. Tell us what the event needs and we will either bring it, coordinate it with a vendor we work with regularly, or tell you straight away that it is outside what we do. All three of those answers are faster than a week of quotes.

FAQ

Equipment list questions

Can I rent one item from the equipment list on its own?
No. Everything on this list goes out as part of a production package that includes delivery, setup, tuning, an operator for the event and strike afterward. That is because placement, gain structure and having someone on the system during the event are what decide whether a room sounds right, and none of that arrives in a road case. If you need a single item for an afternoon, tell us and we will point you toward a company that does counter rental.
Can I pick the equipment up myself?
No, there is no customer pickup. We deliver to the venue, build the system inside the load-in window, tune it to the room, run it for the event and take it away afterward. Your team does not touch a cable at any point in that.
Why are there no quantities on this equipment list?
Because our inventory turns over as we buy, sell and replace boxes, so a number printed on a web page would be out of date within a season. We size the rig to your room on the quote instead. The one exception is the line array, where we own twelve DAS Audio Aero 12 boxes and ten LX218CA subwoofers, because the box count is part of what you are comparing when you get line array quotes.
Do you own this equipment or sub-rent it?
We own it. The system that arrives has been used by our crew in rooms like yours, including the line array and its flying hardware, so we already know how it behaves when a venue's power is not what the floor plan promised. The two exceptions are LED video wall, which we coordinate through vendors we work with regularly, and large CO2 cylinders, which come from a gas supplier. We tell you which is which on the quote.
Do you rent inflatable or outdoor movie screens?
No. We do not carry inflatable screens or outdoor movie screens in any size. Our projection equipment is built for indoor and covered spaces where we can control the ambient light, including an ambient light rejecting screen for rooms that cannot be made dark.
What if the equipment I need is not on this list?
Ask anyway. This list is what we own, and we regularly bring in staging, backline and specialty items through vendors we have worked with for years, quoted and run as part of the same production. If it is genuinely outside what we do, we will say so the same day rather than let you wait on it.
Do you deliver across Dallas-Fort Worth?
Yes. We are based in Hurst, roughly halfway between Dallas and Fort Worth, and work within about forty miles of it: Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Frisco, Arlington, Irving, Southlake, McKinney, Denton, Grapevine, Richardson, Garland and everywhere in between. If your venue is further out, ask anyway and we will tell you honestly whether it works.
Is an operator included with the equipment?
Yes, on every booking. A technician builds the system, line-checks it, sets the microphone levels and stays on it for the event, handling levels, music, microphone changes and the handoffs between segments. For larger events we add a monitor engineer, a stage hand and a lighting operator. The person who tuned the system in the afternoon is the person behind it in the evening.
