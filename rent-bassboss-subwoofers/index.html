How many subwoofers does your event need?

This is the question almost everyone asks first, and the honest answer starts with three things: how many people are in the room, what the music is, and whether there are walls. A hundred guests in a hotel ballroom and a hundred guests in an open backyard need different amounts of low end, because a ballroom keeps the bass and a backyard gives it away. Below is how we size a rig in practice. Tell us the venue and the guest count and we will tell you which line you are on.

Up to 100 guestsBackyard, small hall, house party

Rig 1 sub · 2 tops on poles · one 20 amp circuit

One single 18 inch sub under a pair of tops covers a room this size with room to spare. A single sub also keeps the load in small, which matters in a backyard where everything crosses a lawn. The trap at this size is the opposite of what people expect: it is rarely too little bass, it is a rig set too loud for a small space, which is why we set the level with someone standing at the far end of the room rather than at the mixer.

100 to 250 guestsBallroom, brewery, larger reception

Rig 2 subs · 2 to 4 tops · separate power for the subs

Two subs is the size most DFW receptions and corporate parties land on. Running a pair rather than one is not only about output. Two boxes placed together couple, which means the low end gets deeper and more even across the dance floor instead of loud in one spot and thin in another. This is also the point where the subs want their own circuit, and we advance that with the venue before the date rather than finding out at load in.

250 to 500 guestsClub night, band show, university event

Rig 2 to 4 subs · 4 tops or a small array · dedicated power drop

At this size the room is usually loud enough that the bass has to work through a crowd, and bodies absorb low end. Four subs across the front of a stage give a deep, even field rather than a hot spot in the middle. If the room is long, this is also where we start talking about where the tops go, because a typical pole mount stops being enough once people at the back cannot hear the vocal over the kick.

500 guests and upFestival, warehouse, outdoor stage

Rig 4 or more subs · line array tops · distro and a generator plan

Outdoors there is nothing to reflect the low end back at you, so the number of boxes goes up quickly and the arrangement matters as much as the count. Subs can be stacked in a block, spread across the stage front or arranged so the bass stays on the crowd and off the neighbors. That last one comes up more than people think in Dallas, and it is worth planning before a noise complaint plans it for you. Larger events also move to line array speakers on top.

Bass heavy genresEDM, dubstep, drum and bass, rap and hip hop

Rig Add one sub per two tops · or step up a box size

Guest count is only half the sizing question. A jazz trio and a dubstep set with the same hundred people in the room want very different low end, because in one the bass supports the music and in the other the bass is the music. For those genres we either add subs or move to a bigger box rather than pushing a small rig harder, since a sub that is working at its limit stops sounding deep and starts sounding compressed. What those genres want is impact that stays clean at level, rather than a small rig pushed harder.

Not sure which line you are on?

Send us the venue name, the date and a rough head count. We know most DFW rooms, and if we do not know yours we will ask you for two photos and tell you honestly what it needs. That intake takes about five minutes and it is the difference between a rig that fits and a rig that does not.

The boxes

Why we run BASSBOSS subwoofers and tops

We settled on BASSBOSS for the DFW rental fleet for reasons that show up on a job rather than on a spec sheet. Every box is self-powered, so the amplifier and processing live inside the cabinet: fewer road cases, a faster load in, and no amp rack sitting in a back hallway waiting to overheat. They hold their level through a full night instead of tightening up after the first hour, and they are voiced for the bass heavy music most of our clients book. If you want the longer version, we wrote one: why we use BASSBOSS speakers and subwoofers for Dallas events.

These are the four boxes we rent most. You do not need to pick one. Tell us the room and the music and we will tell you which of these belongs under it.

Top

BASSBOSS DV12 speakers

Box Single 12 inch powered top · pole mount · singles, pairs or a 2x2 array

The DV12 is the top box that goes above the subs on most of our jobs. It carries vocals and music cleanly at distance and keeps its pattern controlled, so a long room does not turn into a bright front and a muddy back. It scales without changing the sound: one for a small room, a pair for most receptions, four in a 2x2 arrangement when a room is wide. Because it is powered, adding a box means adding a box, not rebuilding an amp rack.

Best for Mobile DJs, receptions, corporate general sessions and any room where the tops have to reach the back.

Sub

BASSBOSS SSP118 subwoofers

Box Single 18 inch powered sub · the standard building block

The SSP118 is the sub we rent most, and it is the one we mean when we say one sub or two subs above. A single 18 inch box that reaches deeper and holds level better than a typical rental sub of the same size, which usually means fewer boxes on the truck for the same result in the room. It is also the easiest sub here to scale: start with one, add a second when the room grows, and you get more impact without the character of the low end changing.

Best for Weddings, private parties, mobile DJ work, corporate events and smaller venues.

Sub

BASSBOSS VS21 subwoofers

Box Single 21 inch powered sub · deep extension with punch

A single 21 inch driver moves considerably more air than an 18, and the VS21 uses it for two things at once: it goes deeper, and it keeps the transient impact that makes a kick drum feel physical rather than just loud. That combination is why it is our default when the booking is a club night, a bass heavy DJ set or a band with a serious rhythm section. It stays musical when it is being pushed instead of pumping audibly.

Best for EDM, dubstep, drum and bass, rap and hip hop, and live bands on a real stage.

Sub

BASSBOSS ZV28 subwoofers

Box Dual 18 inch powered sub · the largest box we deploy routinely

The ZV28 is what we bring when the low end has to reach a crowd rather than a room: two 18 inch drivers in one cabinet, reaching low frequencies consistently and staying controlled at a level where a typical sub has already turned into a rumble. It is the headline box on this list, and the deep, clean bottom it produces is the reason a festival or a warehouse show is a different conversation from a wedding. Across a floor that size the impact stays even, so the back of the crowd feels what the front feels.

Best for Festivals, warehouse shows, large outdoor stages and big dance floors.

Concerts and festivals

Concert sound system rental in Dallas

Rig Line array or point source tops · sub block · monitors · engineer

A concert is not a bigger party. It has an input list, a stage plot, a support act, a changeover and someone whose whole job is monitors. When a promoter or a band asks us for a concert sound system in Dallas, what they are really asking for is a rig that holds up at show level for four hours and a crew who has run a changeover before. The BASSBOSS boxes described above are what we build that rig from.

The low end is where most rented concert systems fall down, because they are sized for the room and not for the crowd. A room absorbs very little; five hundred people absorb a great deal. We size the sub count against the crowd and the genre, arrange the boxes so the bass lands on the audience rather than the back of the stage, and keep headroom in hand so the system is not working flat out during the headliner. On a typical festival stage that headroom is the difference between impact and distortion by the third act.

For loudspeakers above the subs, most shows past a few hundred people move to line array speaker rental, and we quote the two together because splitting them across vendors is how a system ends up untuned. If you are booking a band rather than a festival, the live music and band events page covers backline, monitors and input lists.

Clubs, raves and warehouse shows

Club and rave sound system rental

Dallas has a real underground scene, and the rooms it happens in are almost never built for sound. Warehouses, empty retail units, patios, art spaces and rooms with a concrete floor and a metal ceiling all have the same problem: enormous reflections and no bass control. A rig that sounds enormous at the door can be unlistenable in the middle of the floor, and adding volume makes it worse rather than better.

Warehouse and open shell rooms

Hard walls send everything back at you. We aim the tops down onto the crowd instead of across the room, keep the subs off the corners where they load up, and accept a slightly lower level for a much deeper one. Bass in a warehouse comes from arrangement, not from turning it up.

Club installs and takeovers

Plenty of DFW venues have a house system that is fine for a bar night and short for a headliner. We can supplement the house rig with subs and tops for the night, then take it back out, so the venue keeps its install and the promoter gets the show they sold.

Neighbors, curfews and complaints

Low frequencies travel further than anything else and they travel through walls. Where the room has neighbors we arrange the subs to keep the energy on the dance floor and off the property line, and we set a level plan for the night at load in rather than at midnight.

The DJ booth is part of the rig

A booth with no monitoring means a DJ mixing off the room, which is always late. Booth monitors, a clean feed and decks that are already tested are included in the setup. CDJ rental covers the decks themselves.

Lights, lasers and haze on the same call

Most of these shows want more than sound. Lasers, moving heads and a hazer to make the beams visible all come from the same crew and the same truck.

Power for an unbuilt room

Warehouses often have power that was never meant for a show. We work out the available circuits during a walkthrough, bring distro, and plan for a generator when the building genuinely cannot carry it.

Breweries, barns and event hallsReceptions, launches, community events

These rooms are long, hard surfaced and usually have a bar at the opposite end from the dance floor. One rig aimed at everything makes the dance floor painful and the bar inaudible, so we split coverage and keep the subs on the floor rather than trying to serve both ends from one stack.

Warehouses and raw spacesRaves, art events, pop ups

Rig 4 or more subs · tops aimed down · distro or generator

Bars and patiosResidencies, small live music, DJ nights

A typical bar room wants a rig that gives the dance floor real low end without stopping conversation six feet away at the bar. That is a placement and tuning job more than an equipment one, and it is where a tuned rental beats a bigger untuned one every time.

Subwoofer rental questions we get asked

How many subwoofers do I need for my event?
It depends on guest count, music style, and whether the room is enclosed. As a rough guide, one sub covers up to about 100 guests indoors, two subs suit most receptions of 100 to 250, and 250 guests and up usually wants four or more. Bass heavy genres and outdoor events both push that number higher. Tell us the venue and the head count and we will size it properly.
Do you deliver and set up subwoofer rentals in Dallas and Fort Worth?
Yes. Every rental is full-service. We deliver, place and tune the rig, run it for the length of your event, then strike and load out. Delivery, setup, tuning and pickup are all included rather than added.
What areas do you serve around Dallas-Fort Worth?
We commonly serve Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Irving, Grapevine, Plano, Frisco, Richardson and Denton, within roughly a forty mile radius of Hurst. Outside that, ask about availability and we will tell you honestly whether it works for your date.
Do you allow customer pickup?
No. Dallas Event Audio is a full-service company, so delivery, setup and pickup are part of every rental. That is also why the rig arrives tuned and working rather than as a pile of boxes.
Can I rent subwoofers only and use my own tops or the venue PA?
Yes. We regularly put rented BASSBOSS subs under a band's own PA, a DJ's tops or a venue's house system. Send us the make and model of your tops at intake and we will match the crossover and gain to them on site.
Do you rent BASSBOSS speakers as well as subwoofers?
Yes. We rent BASSBOSS DV12 powered tops individually, in pairs or as a 2x2 array, alongside the SSP118, VS21 and ZV28 subs. Tops and subs are usually quoted together so the system is matched.
Which subwoofer suits deep bass and dubstep drops?
For the deepest extension on a large floor, the ZV28. For deep bass with hard transient impact, the VS21. For a powerful single 18 inch building block that scales as the room grows, the SSP118. We will recommend one after hearing the venue and the set.
Are these systems good for EDM, house, drum and bass, rap and hip hop?
Yes. The fleet is chosen for bass heavy music, with deep extension and the transient impact that makes a kick feel physical rather than only loud. These are the same rigs we run for club nights and warehouse shows across DFW.
Do I need to supply power for a subwoofer rental?
Usually the venue's power is enough, but it has to be the right circuits rather than whatever is nearest. We advance power with the venue before the date and bring distro when the room needs it. For warehouses and outdoor sites we will plan for a generator.
How far in advance should I book a subwoofer rental?
Two to four weeks is comfortable for most events, and longer for peak wedding and holiday dates. Short notice is often still possible, so call us rather than assuming it is too late.
