The Part Most People Skip

Licensed, insured, and FDA variance compliant.

Laser light shows are regulated. Every Dallas Event Audio laser show is operated by an approved FDA-variance holder. That is the reason we can legally put beams over a crowd.

Before a single beam goes up, we work through the same review every time:

1

Venue review: ceiling height, rigging points, sightlines, and where the beams can safely live.

2

Audience area assessment: mapping where guests will be standing, sitting, and moving all night.

3

Beam path planning: scan zones and termination points set so the show stays well clear of eye level.

4

Airspace coordination: required for outdoor shows, handled by us before your event date.

Common Questions

Laser show questions, answered straight.

Do I need a permit for a laser light show?
Laser light shows require an FDA/CDRH variance to operate legally in the United States. You don't need to obtain one yourself. We hold the variance and handle the compliance side as part of the booking, including the paperwork your venue may ask for.
Can laser light shows be used outdoors?
Yes. Our 40W systems are designed specifically for festivals and large outdoor venues. Outdoor shows add one step, airspace coordination, which we take care of ahead of your event date.
Is a laser light show safe for guests?
Yes, when operated by a trained technician with properly rated equipment, which is why we staff every laser rental with our own operator. Beam paths and audience scanning zones are planned before the event, not improvised during it.
What's the difference between 5W, 10W, and 40W lasers?
Power determines brightness, how far the beams stay visible, and how much space the show can cover. A 5W system is right for an indoor room of around 200 guests; 10W handles most receptions and galas up to roughly 1,000; 40W is for large venues and open-air productions where the beams have to travel.
Do you deliver and set up, or do I pick the equipment up?
We deliver, set up, run, and strike everything ourselves. There's no customer pickup and no self-service on laser rentals, an on-site technician is part of every laser booking, both for safety and because the show is programmed and run live.
