The Patterns

What comes up again and again in the reviews

Read enough of them in one sitting and the same handful of things keep surfacing. Each one links to the rental or service page that explains how we do it.

Reading the room

Across all six platforms, the line that keeps coming back is some version of reading the crowd. That is the part of DJ services that no rental inventory can promise for you, and it is why our DJs work the floor rather than the playlist.

The gear was better than expected

We own what we send out, so the speakers, subwoofers and consoles that show up are the ones we have run at the last dozen events. Our full wedding ceremony and reception sound rental packages and the wider audio services both come off the same inventory.

Microphones that behave

Reviewers mention vows and toasts carrying to the back row. Every one of our microphone rentals includes the AI powered De-Feedback system, which is why a lapel on an officiant does not have to be rung out or ridden down.

Timelines that move

Several reviews describe the timeline changing on the day and nobody panicking. That is easier when the DJ and the rental crew are the same people, which is how we run every wedding and every corporate event.

Lighting the room

Uplighting comes up in the wedding reviews more than any other lighting item, usually color matched to the linens. Uplighting rental sits inside our lighting services alongside dance floor lighting, follow spot and monogram projection.

Showing up early

Punctual, showed up early, set up before guests arrived. That is what full service rental means here, and our story explains why we have never done customer pickup. You can see the finished rooms in our work.

Want to leave one?

If we have worked your event, the Google, WeddingWire, The Knot, Yelp, Zola and Facebook profiles linked above are all open for reviews. It helps the next couple or planner decide, and we read every one of them.

Questions

Common questions about our reviews

Where can I read Dallas Event Audio reviews?
On Google, WeddingWire, The Knot, Yelp, Zola and Facebook. Every one of those profiles is linked from the platform cards near the top of this page, and each link opens the full listing so you can read complete reviews rather than the excerpts here.
How many reviews does Dallas Event Audio have?
About 194 public reviews across the six platforms as of the day this page was built. That is 89 on WeddingWire, 44 on The Knot, 43 on Google, 12 on Yelp, 3 on Zola and 3 on Facebook. Yelp holds back another 123 on its not recommended list, which is linked near the top of this page. The counts move as new ones come in, so treat the number on the platform as the current one.
Where are most Dallas Event Audio reviews posted?
WeddingWire, with 89 reviews at 4.9 out of 5 and a 98 percent recommend rate. The Knot has the longest run of five star reviews at 44, and Google carries the widest mix of corporate, private and live music work alongside weddings.
What is The Knot Best of Weddings award?
The Knot gives it each year to vendors whose client reviews place them at the top of their category. We have received it three times, in 2021, 2022 and 2023. It is scored from what clients wrote, so it reflects the reviews rather than sitting apart from them.
Do you have reviews from corporate and private event clients?
Yes, and most of them are on Google, since the wedding platforms only accept wedding reviews. Company holiday parties, conferences, veterinary and medical education events, fundraisers and private parties all show up there. The corporate and private section above pulls several of them together.
Do the same people handle the sound and the DJ?
Yes. We are a sound company that also DJs, so the speakers, the microphones and the person on the decks all come from one crew on one phone number, and the sound system rental is part of the same booking. Reviewers tend to notice it as the timeline holding together and nobody arguing about the room.
Can you provide a certificate of insurance for my venue?
Yes. We carry liability insurance and can send a certificate naming your venue as additional insured, usually the same day you ask. Most Dallas-Fort Worth venues want it a week or two ahead of load in, and we handle that directly with the venue if you prefer.
How do I get a quote for my event?
Send us the date, the venue and roughly what you need, through the contact page or by phone. We will come back the same day with a straightforward quote for the sound, lighting, DJ or video rental the event calls for.
