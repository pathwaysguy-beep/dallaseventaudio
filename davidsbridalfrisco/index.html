Before you ask

The questions every bride asks.

Will my venue allow it?

Almost always yes, and a lot of DFW venues have already seen us do it. Send us your venue name and we'll tell you within the day. If they need paperwork or a certificate, we handle that conversation for you.

Is the floor slippery afterward?

Your floor stays good to dance on. Here's the honest version: with heavy use it can end up slightly damp, and that's exactly why walking the floor is built into our setup. It happens at every event, before your guests come back on.

What about the smoke alarm?

A dry ice cloud is chilled water vapor: the same thing you see over a cold drink, just a lot more of it. In twenty years of DFW weddings it has never set an alarm off. We still coordinate with your venue ahead of time and place the machine for the room, because that's how it stays a non-issue.

How long does the cloud last?

It depends on how hard the machine is run for your room, so instead of a minute count we start from which moments you want covered (just the first dance, or the entrance and the last song too) and match the machine above to that.

Do we have to book you as our DJ?

No. We're not a DJ. We work alongside whoever you've already hired, and if your DJ, band, or traveling musician needs sound gear, we can supply that too.
