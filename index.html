AV Rental Dallas

AV equipment rental across Dallas-Fort Worth, run by the people who own it.

Sound, lighting, projection and effects from one company, on one truck, with one technician who stays for the whole event.

Most people arrive here searching for AV rental in Dallas, or for audio visual equipment rental covering one specific room on one specific date. What they book is closer to event audio visual services than to a rental counter: the speakers, the lighting, the projection and the effects are specified together, delivered together, and run by an insured AV technician from load-in to breakdown. We bring it in and we take it back out.

Most bookings start with sound system rental. PA speakers sized to the room rather than to a catalog, subwoofers when the dance floor needs low end you can feel, monitors for the people on the microphone, and a line check before doors open. Speaker rental with us means the system is tuned to the room you are standing in, at the volume that room wants. The full inventory sits on the sound equipment rental page, with the touring-grade end of it on the line array and subwoofer pages.

Weddings get their own treatment, because wedding sound equipment rental is really three audio events wearing one name. A ceremony that has to carry vows outdoors, a cocktail hour that has to sit under conversation, and a reception that has to hold a full dance floor are three different rigs, and we spec them as three. Every microphone in the ceremony rig carries our AI-powered De-Feedback system as standard, so the vows land without a squeal. The wedding AV page walks through the day hour by hour.

Corporate event AV in Dallas fills the rest of the calendar: conferences, general sessions, awards nights, panels and product launches, where the audio has to be invisible and the slides have to be readable from the back row. Live sound services, projection and screens, presenter microphones and stage lighting come from the same crew and the same load-in, which is the whole reason it goes quietly. See corporate events for how a typical day is built.

Booking event lighting and sound rental together is the norm here rather than the exception, because the cues line up when one crew owns both. Before You Ask

The questions we get most.

If yours is not here, ask us directly and we answer the same day.

What areas do you serve?
We cover the whole Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex: Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Frisco, Irving, Southlake, Grapevine, McKinney, Denton, Richardson and the mid-cities, roughly forty miles out from our base in Hurst. Further out is usually still possible; ask us with the venue address and we will tell you straight away.
What is included in a DJ booking?
The DJ, the sound system, the microphones and the dance-floor lighting, delivered, set up, line-checked and run by our technician from load-in to breakdown. You are not renting a person and then sourcing the gear separately. The detail is on the DJ services page.
Do you rent equipment without a DJ?
Yes. If you have already hired your DJ, or the host is running the music themselves, we supply the rig on its own: media players, mixer, controller, DJ booth, speakers and subwoofers. It still arrives delivered, built and line-checked by our technician rather than handed over at a counter. See DJ equipment rental.
Do you offer DJ equipment rental in Dallas?
We do, across the whole Metroplex. DJ equipment rental with us means the gear is delivered, set up, tested and staffed. Our crew brings it in, rigs it and stays with it. Full list of what you can rent is on the DJ equipment rental page, and the wider audio inventory is on sound equipment rental.
How far in advance should I book?
Peak Saturdays in spring and autumn go early, so six to nine months out is comfortable for a wedding and three months for most corporate dates. That said, we keep inventory for short notice more often than people expect, so if your date is close, ask anyway.
Do you provide lighting and uplighting separately?
Yes. Uplighting, dance-floor and stage lighting, monograms, pin spots and lasers can be booked on their own or added to an audio booking, which is the more common route because it means one crew and one load-in. The hub is lighting services.
What is Dancing on the Clouds?
A low-lying fog effect for a first dance or a grand entrance. The fog sits at ankle height instead of filling the room, so the couple appears to be standing on a cloud and the photographs come out beautifully. Details on the Dancing on the Clouds page.
How do I get a quote?
Tell us the date, the venue and roughly how many guests, and we will come back with a straightforward quote. Tell us about your event and we'll send you a straightforward quote the same day. Contact us here or call 817-210-7957.
What does AV equipment rental include?
Delivery, setup, a line check, an insured AV technician on site for the whole event, and breakdown at the end. An AV rental from us is sound, lighting, projection and effects specified as one system rather than as separate line items, and the same technician who sets it up is the one running it when your first speaker walks up. Start on the sound equipment rental page and we will fill in the rest around your room.
Do you handle AV for corporate events and conferences?
Yes. Conferences, general sessions, awards nights, panels, product launches and multi-day meetings. That usually means a sound system sized to the room, presenter and lapel microphones carrying our De-Feedback system, projection and screens bright enough for the actual light in the room, and stage lighting, all from one crew and one load-in. The corporate events page has the detail.
