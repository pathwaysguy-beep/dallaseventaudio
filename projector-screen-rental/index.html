The short version

Projector and screen rental, matched as one package

Most people searching for a projector rental in Dallas need a screen with it, and the two have to agree with each other. A bright projector aimed at the wrong screen surface in a room with the lights on gives you a washed out image, and a beautiful screen fed by an underpowered projector gives you the same result. So we rent them as a set. You tell us the room and the audience size, and the projector and screen come out of our inventory already paired for that job.

Every setup goes out the same way. Our crew delivers it, rigs the screen, dresses the cable, connects your laptop, tunes the image for the light in the room, and stays long enough to confirm your first slide looks right on the screen. Then we come back and pick it all up. There is no counter pickup and nothing is left in a parking lot for your team to carry.

Package one

Ultra short throw projector rental and ambient light rejecting screens for bright rooms

Rig Epson PowerLite 700U · 150 inch CLR screen · ultra short throw

This is the setup for a room you cannot darken. The Epson PowerLite 700U is a 4,000 lumen WUXGA laser projector that sits inches from the screen instead of across the room, and it is paired with an Elite Screens Light-On CLR StarBright ambient light rejecting screen at 150 inches. The screen surface is built to reject the light coming down from the ceiling and accept only the light coming up from the projector, so the image holds its contrast with the house lights on.

Because the throw distance is inches rather than feet, nobody walks through the beam and casts a shadow across your slide, and you do not lose the middle of the room to a projector stand. Lobbies, hotel prefunction space, retail activations, reception areas and glass-walled conference rooms are all rooms we bring this setup to. Clients stop asking us whether they can dim the lights, because the answer no longer matters.

Package two

Long throw projector rental for ballrooms and larger rooms

When the room can go dark and the projector can live behind the audience or on a truss, a long throw setup gives you more placement freedom and a bigger image for the same money.

Rig Epson Pro EX10000 · 124 or 144 inch screen · long throw

The Epson Pro EX10000 is a 4,600 lumen Full HD laser projector with wireless and Miracast input, paired with either a 124 inch tab tensioned tripod screen or a 144 inch white matte screen depending on how far back the room lets us sit. Laser means no lamp warm up, no lamp to fail in the middle of a keynote, and the same brightness at the end of a long conference day as at the start of it.

This is the workhorse for hotel ballrooms, banquet rooms, general session rooms, worship spaces and any room with real seating depth. Give us the ceiling height, the throw distance and the seating layout and we will tell you which of the two screens fits, then bring the one that does. Larger rooms sometimes want two screens so the far side of the audience is not reading at an angle, and we rent projectors and screens in pairs for that reason.

Brightness

How many lumens does your room need?

Lumens is the number people ask about, but the honest answer is that lumens and screen surface are one decision, not two.

Room you can dim

3,000 to 4,000 lumens is enough for a ballroom or meeting room where the house lights come down and the shades close. Both of our projectors clear that comfortably, so in a dimmed room the screen size drives the choice rather than the brightness.

Windows or lights on

A room with daylight or full house lights wants 5,000 lumens and up, or a projector paired with an ambient light rejecting screen, which is the route we take. The CLR screen buys back more contrast than the extra lumens would, and it does it without a projector the size of a suitcase in the middle of your room.

Screen surface does half the work

Two setups can put the same lumens on the wall and look nothing alike. Matte white spreads light evenly for wide seating, and the Light-On CLR surface rejects overhead light so the image survives with the lights up. We pick the surface for the room, then pick the projector that is paired to it.

We check it in your room

Brightness on paper is a starting point. On the day we set the image, check it from the back row and the outside seats, and adjust before your first presenter walks up. If your room turns out brighter than the floor plan suggested, that is our problem to solve at setup, not yours.

Questions

Projector rental questions we get asked in Dallas-Fort Worth

Do you rent the projector and the screen together?
Yes. We rent projectors and screens together as matched packages, and that is how most people book it. Renting them as a set gives the better result, because the projector and the screen are paired for the size and the light level of your room rather than picked separately and hoped for.
How many lumens do I need to rent a projector for my event?
For a room you can dim, 3,000 to 4,000 lumens is usually enough. A room with windows or the house lights up wants 5,000 lumens and above, or a projector paired with an ambient light rejecting screen, which is the route we normally take. Tell us your room and your audience size and we will match the projector and screen to it.
What size projector screen do I need?
A rough guide by audience: about 50 guests wants a 6 to 8 foot screen, about 100 wants 8 to 10 foot, about 150 wants 10 to 12 foot, and 200 or more wants 12 to 16 foot. Deeper rooms and longer viewing distances push the screen bigger. We rent screens up to 150 inches and check the size against your floor plan before the truck loads.
Can I use a projector in a bright room or with the lights on?
Yes. Our ultra short throw Epson PowerLite 700U paired with the Light-On CLR ambient light rejecting screen keeps the image sharp with overhead and ambient light in the room. That setup is what we bring for lobbies, receptions, glass-walled conference rooms and any space you cannot darken.
Can I connect my laptop or phone?
Yes. Our projectors connect over HDMI for laptops and most devices, and the Epson Pro EX10000 also supports wireless and Miracast. We bring the cables and the adapters, connect your machine at setup and test the whole signal path before we leave.
Do you deliver and set it up?
Yes. We deliver, rig the screen, set the projector, tune the image and pick everything up across the Dallas-Fort Worth metro, and we can staff an on-site technician for the event. Dallas Event Audio is a full-service company, so a projector rental arrives set up and working.
Do you have projector rental for a wedding?
Yes. We provide projectors and dressed screens for wedding slideshows, ceremony visuals and reception displays, with the cable dressed and the frame skirted so the screen looks right in the photographs. Ask about pairing it with monogram projection if you also want your initials on the floor or a wall.
Do you do corporate projector rental for conferences?
Yes, and corporate meetings are most of what this inventory does. A corporate setup usually pairs the projector and screen with microphones, speakers and a recording or streaming feed, all on one quote, and we advance the load in with the hotel or venue before the date.
How far in advance should I book a projector rental?
One to two weeks ahead when you can. Weekend dates and the peak spring and fall corporate season fill up first. We will confirm availability and hold the gear for your event once the quote is approved.
How much does it cost to rent a projector in Dallas?
Projector and screen rentals are quoted per event, based on which setup the room needs, the screen size, the length of the booking and whether you want an on-site technician. Send us the venue, the date and the audience size and we will quote it the same day.
Do you rent projectors without a screen?
We can, and sometimes a venue already owns a good fixed screen. Tell us the screen surface, its size and where it lives in the room, and we will bring the projector that is paired to it. In every other case we would rather send the set, because a projector on a mismatched surface is a common way a good image goes wrong.

Tell us the room and the audience size.

Tell us about your event and we'll send you a straightforward quote the same day. Venue, date, audience size and how much light you can kill, and we will come back with the projector and screen that fit the room.
