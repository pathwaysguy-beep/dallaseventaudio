Ceremony Sound Rentals

The vows are the one part you can't do over.

A ceremony is the hardest audio of the whole day and the least forgiving: quiet voices, an outdoor space with no walls to help, and one take. We treat it that way.

1

Everyone hears it, including the back row. Speakers placed for the seating plan you have, not aimed at the first three rows. Outdoors we plan for wind and open air; indoors we tune to the room so it stays intelligible instead of just loud.

2

Wireless handheld and lapel mics, with AI De-Feedback standard. Every wireless microphone rental includes our AI-powered feedback-suppression system as standard. The officiant, the readings and the vows all come through clean.

3

Live musicians get their own inputs. A string trio, a solo guitarist, a harpist, a vocalist. We mic and reinforce them properly instead of hoping a laptop speaker carries it, and we run the balance live as the processional starts.

4

Cues are run by a person at the board. Our technician is there for the processional, the pause, the kiss, and the recessional. If the wedding party is thirty seconds late, the music waits with them.

5

Then it moves to the reception. The same team strikes the ceremony rig and has cocktail hour and the reception up before your guests find their seats.

Common Questions

Wedding AV questions, answered straight.

Do you deliver and set up, or do we pick the equipment up?
We deliver, set up, run, and strike everything ourselves. An insured technician is on-site from load-in through the last song. You never touch a cable, and nobody in your wedding party gets handed a job on the day.
Can you cover an outdoor ceremony with no power?
Yes. Outdoor ceremonies are routine for us. We plan for the distance, the wind, and the power situation ahead of time, including battery-powered options where there's no outlet. Tell us where the aisle is and we'll tell you what it takes.
What about microphone feedback during the vows and toasts?
Every microphone rental includes our AI-powered De-Feedback system as standard. It suppresses feedback before it becomes the squeal everyone remembers, which matters most in the two moments you can't redo: the vows and the toasts.
We're bringing our own DJ or band. Can you just provide the gear?
That's a big part of what we do. Traveling wedding DJs get CDJs, mixers, PA, subs, booth and facade; live wedding bands get front-of-house, monitors, subs, stands, cables, and a console on request. It's all set up, line-checked, and staffed. Your DJ or band walks into a working system.
How far in advance should we book?
Peak DFW wedding dates, spring and fall Saturdays, get claimed first, often a year out. If you have a date and a venue, send them over and we'll tell you honestly whether we're open. If we're not, we'll say so rather than stringing you along.
Do you handle the ceremony and the reception, or just one?
Either, but most couples book both. It's one delivery, one setup crew, and one point of contact instead of coordinating two vendors across the same afternoon. We strike the ceremony rig and have cocktail hour and the reception up while your guests are still finding their tables.
