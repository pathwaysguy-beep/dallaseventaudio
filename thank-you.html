Next Steps

What happens now

Three steps, and the first one starts as soon as your request lands in the inbox.

Step one

We read it

A person on our crew reads your request and checks your date and venue against the calendar. If we are already booked that day we tell you right away so you can keep looking.

Step two

We ask anything we still need

If the room, the timing or the guest count changes what we would bring, you get one short round of questions by email or a quick call. We would rather ask than guess at the equipment list.

Step three

You get a written quote

The quote lists the equipment for your event and the on-site technician who delivers it, sets it up, runs it through the night and strikes it. Read it, ask questions, change it. It stays a quote until you tell us to hold the date.

What speeds the quote up

If you already know these, reply to the confirmation email with them and we can skip a round of questions.

The six things that shape an equipment list

The venue and its address tell us the size of the room, the power situation and the load in. The date and your on-site times tell us how long the system is up and who is running it. A rough guest count tells us how much sound the room needs. Whether you are indoors or outdoors changes the speakers, the lighting and the weather plan. And any venue rules on load in, noise limits or curfew are worth sending early, because they are the ones that surprise people on the day.

If you do not have all of it yet, send what you have. We quote from partial information all the time and adjust as the plan firms up. For a longer walk through of how a booking goes, see how we work.
