DJ Services Dallas

What you are hiring when you hire a DJ.

There are two very different things sold under the same words in this city. One is a person and a playlist. The other is a person, a playlist, a system that fills the room evenly, microphones that do not squeal during the toasts, lighting that changes when the night changes, and a technician standing behind all of it. Dallas Event Audio sells the second version.

The difference is structural. We started as an AV company and we still are one. The line array, the subwoofers, the microphones and the lighting rig are ours, sitting in our warehouse in the mid-cities. When you book DJ services from us, none of it is subcontracted and nothing is being picked up on the way to your venue.

The practical version: one contract, one load-in, one crew, one person to call if the timeline moves. Nobody on your side ever touches a cable, and nothing arrives at a loading dock for you to sort out.

Choosing Between DJs In Dallas

There are a lot of DJs in Dallas. Here is how to tell them apart.

Search “DJ services near me” from anywhere in the Metroplex and you will get a long list, and every DJ on it will say yes to your date and sound confident about your room. These are the four questions that separate them, and they are worth asking of every DJ on that list, including us.

Question 01

Whose speakers are those?

If the answer is “a rental house,” then the system arriving at your venue is one your DJ has never turned on before, chosen from a price list rather than for your room. Every system we bring is our own. We know how a given rig behaves in a ballroom with a hard ceiling versus a tent versus a converted warehouse, because we have put it in all three.

Why it mattersNobody discovers your room’s problems at 6 p.m. on the day.
Question 02

Who is standing there when a microphone feeds back?

A DJ working alone has to choose between the mix and the problem. We staff events so there is a technician on the room as well as a DJ on the music, and every microphone we send out carries our AI-powered De-Feedback system as standard. It catches the ring before a room full of people hears it. There is a longer explanation of how it works if you are curious.

Why it mattersToasts and vows are the part nobody gets a second take on.
Question 03

What happens when the timeline slips?

It always slips. The dinner runs long, the photographer needs ten more minutes, the speech nobody planned for happens anyway. What you want is a DJ who has run enough Dallas-Fort Worth events since 2001 to absorb a twenty-minute shift without the room noticing anything changed.

Why it mattersYour guests should never see the seams.
Question 04

Can they send your venue a certificate of insurance?

Increasingly your venue will ask before they let anyone through the door, and a surprising number of DJs cannot produce one. Dallas Event Audio is fully insured and used to venue paperwork. Send us the requirements with your inquiry and we handle it directly with the coordinator.

Why it mattersIt is the least glamorous reason a booking falls apart.

What Comes With The DJ

The DJ is the part you see. This is the rest of it.

Booking DJ services from an AV company means the things most DJs quote as extras are what shows up. Everything here is delivered, set up, run and struck by our crew.

The sound systemSized to your room

A system chosen for the space rather than whatever fits in the van, from a pair of tops on sticks for a hundred guests to a full line array with BassBoss subwoofers for a ballroom or a warehouse. Coverage is set so the back tables hear the same mix as the dance floor and the head table can still hold a conversation. See the full range on the sound equipment page.

MicrophonesWith De-Feedback standard

Handhelds for toasts, lavaliers for officiants, a podium mic for a keynote, whatever the run sheet needs. Every one of them carries our AI-powered De-Feedback system as standard, which is the difference between a room that hears a speech and a room that hears a squeal. It is not an upgrade; it is on every microphone we own.

DJ equipmentIndustry-standard decks

Pioneer CDJ-class decks and a mixer our DJs work on every weekend, so nothing about the night is spent adapting to unfamiliar equipment. If you have hired your own DJ and only need the gear and the system under them, that is CDJ rental, still delivered and set up by us.

LightingDance floor and room

Moving heads and dance-floor wash so the floor does not sit under house fluorescents, plus uplighting to change the color of the whole room between dinner and dancing. Effects if you want them: lasers, low-lying fog for a first dance, haze so the beams read on camera, a monogram on the wall. The lighting services page has the whole catalog.

A technician on the roomNot just a DJ on the decks

Someone whose whole job is the room rather than the mix: line-checking before doors, watching levels as the space fills and the acoustics change, handing microphones to the right people at the right moment, and fixing the thing that breaks before anyone notices it broke.

Load-in, setup and strikeBefore and after, out of sight

We arrive early enough to be finished and quiet before your guests do, coordinate directly with your venue and planner on the dock and the timeline, and strike after the last guest leaves. The crew that sets it up is the crew that runs it.

Questions

What people ask before they book a DJ.

Is the sound system included, or is that extra?
Included, and it is the main reason to book DJ services from us rather than from a DJ who has to rent one. The system is ours, it is chosen for your room rather than off a price list, and it comes with the microphones, the stands, the cabling and the technician who runs it. What varies is how much system your room needs, and that is what we work out with you before quoting.
How much do DJ services in Dallas cost?
We do not publish prices, because the honest answer depends on the room. A four-hour party for eighty guests and a full-day wedding for four hundred with ceremony audio, uplighting and a second system outdoors are different jobs, and a single number would misprice one of them badly. Send us the date, the venue and the headcount and you will get an itemized quote back, usually the same day, with nothing hidden in it.
Do you take song requests from guests?
Yes, filtered through whatever you tell us in advance. Most clients give us a short must-play list, a firm do-not-play list, and specific songs for the moments that matter, then leave the rest to the DJ reading the floor. If you would rather guests were not approaching the booth at all, say so and we will handle it politely on your behalf.
Can you handle the ceremony as well as the reception?
Regularly, and they are genuinely separate audio problems. A ceremony needs quiet, even coverage and microphones that pick up vows without feedback. Ours all carry our AI-powered De-Feedback system as standard. A reception needs a system that can fill a dance floor. We usually set both, so nothing has to be torn down and rebuilt while your guests are moving between spaces.
Do you bring lighting, or is that a separate booking?
Same booking, same crew, same load-in. Dance-floor lighting is the usual starting point, and from there people add uplighting to change the color of the room, a monogram, lasers, or low-lying fog for a first dance. Because it is on one contract it shares a setup window and one person calling the night, which is the version where the lighting cue and the song land together.
How far in advance should we book a DJ in Dallas-Fort Worth?
Earlier is better for peak spring and fall Saturdays, which get claimed a long way out. That said, we do take short-notice events and we have the inventory to make them work, so if your date is close it is still worth asking. We will tell you straight away whether it is open.
Can we just rent the DJ equipment without a DJ?
Yes, and plenty of clients do when they have a DJ of their own or a band. You get the decks, the system and the microphones set up and line-checked, with a technician on-site for the event. Everything is delivered, set up and run by our own crew, because that is what keeps it working.
Are you insured, and can you send our venue a certificate?
Yes to both. We are fully insured and used to venue paperwork. Send the venue’s requirements along with your inquiry and we will deal with their coordinator directly, which is usually faster than passing documents back and forth through you.
What areas of Dallas-Fort Worth do you cover?
The whole Metroplex, roughly a forty-mile radius from the mid-cities, covering Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Plano, Frisco, Irving, Southlake, McKinney, Richardson, Grapevine, Denton and everywhere folded in between. If your venue is further out, ask anyway and we will tell you honestly whether it works.
